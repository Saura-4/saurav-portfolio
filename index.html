03. Things I've Built

2026 · Evaluation Framework

Modular RAG Evaluation Framework

A from-scratch RAG framework — no LangChain, no LlamaIndex — with interchangeable indexing, chunking, retrieval, reranking, generation, and evaluation modules. Benchmarked 4 chunking strategies against 4 retrieval pipelines on BEIR SciFact, implemented Recall@k, Precision@k, MRR and NDCG@k from scratch, and evaluated CoVe, Cited-Span and Abstain generation with a RAGTruth-inspired hallucination pipeline.
